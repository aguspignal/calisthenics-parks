Brief:

Mobile app for finding nearby calisthenics parks, displaying their location in a 
map. The users load new parks and maintain/update/review existing ones. For each park, the following information is shown in its own detailed screen:
- Location
- Available equipment (pullup bars, parallel bars, etc)
- Photos
- Quality of the equipment
- Ratings (overall, environment, etc)
- Accessibility and amenities (water access, bathrooms nearby, etc)
- Reviews

UI considerations:

- The overall look of the app has to be simple and minimal
- Extract the design system from: https://claude.ai/artifact/PkYEXEUpE6nkx4rNFAeNiT
- The app opens directly in the map, with filters at the top (by equipment, amenities, etc), as well as an access to setting. At the bottom, a button to center at user's location and a "contribute" button
- The contribute screen allows the user to add a new park or review/update an existing one.

Stack:

- Expo React Native + Typescript
- Supabase
